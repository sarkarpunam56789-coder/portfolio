Problem

Climate anxiety is high. Action is low. The gap isn’t motivation, it’s feedback.

The problem

People can’t see how everyday choices add up, so they don’t know what to change.

The idea

Explain environmental data in plain language and turn it into one clear next step.

The vision

One place where anyone can see their impact and know what to do next.

Problems I set out to solve

From my review of existing sustainability tools in version 1, and how version 2 answers each.

  • Data opacityEvery score has a “Why this?” explanation, and the coach explains how it is calculated.
  • No actionabilityA “Your next best action” card sits next to the score on the dashboard.
  • Fragmented dataOne score and one dashboard across transport, energy, water and waste.
  • Poor mobile UXThe dashboard has a mobile layout. Use the Mobile toggle in the prototype.
  • No social layerCommunity stays a product area (leaderboard, team challenges). Its screens are not designed yet.
  • Boring interfaceOne deep green, clear data colours and plain language instead of dense charts.

Concept persona

Maya

A concept persona based on my review of existing tools. Not built from interviews and not validated with real users.

Wants: to know whether her everyday choices matter, and what to do about it.

Gets frustrated by: data without explanation, and tools that make her do all the work.

Where the product helps Maya

The riskiest moment is connecting accounts: it asks for the most trust before she has seen any value. Onboarding in the prototype shows what is read, what never is, and gives her a score before asking for more.

Key decisions

DecisionWhyTrade-off
One Earth score (0–100)A quick read answers “how am I doing?” before any detail.Hides detail, so the breakdown sits one step down.
Next best action beside the scoreA recommendation is more useful than another chart.Needs a visible reason, so every action has a “Why this?”.
Three-step onboardingShort setup lowers the chance people quit before seeing value.Needs clear reassurance about data, so the privacy panel comes first.
Deep green with separate data coloursCalm, readable, and quick to scan: blue for water, amber for waste.Colour is never the only signal, so values and labels always appear.

Product structure

The same five areas as version 1.

Design system

Kept small on purpose: one green, neutral text, three data colours, an 8px spacing scale. Primary green on the off-white background is about 6:1 contrast.

Green #166A43
Forest #12301F
Tint #EAF3E6
Water #2B7BB9
Waste #B9800F
Text #14231B

Plus Jakarta Sans

Headings and key numbers. Body and UI text in Inter, 16px minimum.

Components use 16px radius, 24px padding and 44px minimum tap targets.

What changed from version 1

  • Removed the invented stats, testimonials and unproven research claims.
  • Added a next best action, onboarding with a privacy panel, an AI Coach and a mobile dashboard.
  • Fixed contradictory dashboard data, added units and consistent labels.
  • Cut the repeated hero and dashboard, and the stock photos.

What I would test next

  • Can a new user connect a source and see a score quickly?
  • Do users understand what the Earth score means?
  • Which suggested action do people choose first?
  • Honest limits: not yet tested with users. Community and goals screens, the landing page and the score logic are not part of this version.
